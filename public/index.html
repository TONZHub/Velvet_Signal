The trust contract

A subscription is permission to propose.

Never permission to silently rewrite. The human edition persuades; the machine edition declares exactly what it would add.

01 · InspectNo mystery payloads.

02 · ConsentThe human holds the gate.

WebMCP delivery stays locked until the exact patch has been approved in the interface.

03 · PrecedenceYour words outrank ours.

Newer explicit user instructions always override a publication patch.

Every patch carries a review date so old guidance does not fossilize into truth.
