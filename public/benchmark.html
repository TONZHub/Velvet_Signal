The benchmark already paid for itself

It caught a real retrieval bug.

On the first deterministic run, the query Who won the 1998 World Cup? returned three Pantry claims. Every lexical relevance score was zero, but the core retriever filled the result limit anyway. The answerability layer then saw “current claims” and called the query grounded.

VS-Bench exposed the failure immediately. The zero-score fallback was removed and locked behind a regression test. The same query now returns Claims: none and Answerability: no-current-context.

Having publications in memory does not mean one of them must answer every question.
Why this matters

Without a relevance floor, RAG can become random context injection with confidence. Negative capability is part of the product: Velvet Signal must be able to say “I do not have a current patch for that.”

Why 6/8 is useful

The model still gets to fail.

Retrieval was clean. Dolphin still missed two generation-adherence behaviors. Those failures stay visible instead of being averaged into a vanity score.

No-current-context labeling

Velvet correctly returned no claims for the World Cup question. Dolphin answered from general knowledge but did not explicitly say that knowledge was outside current Velvet Signal context.

Retrieved smell guardrail

In the tight-context scenario, the smell-safety claim was actually retrieved. Dolphin nevertheless treated that facet as uncovered instead of applying the retrieved guardrail. That is a model-adherence miss, not a retrieval miss.

What gets scored

Six separate layers.

01 · StorageDid the patch survive?

Approved patches should remain in the user-controlled ledger across terminal, Ollama, code, and computer restarts.

02 · EligibilityShould this claim be active?

Locked, expired, rejected, replaced, or displaced conflicting claims should never enter active retrieval.

03 · ResolutionWhat changed—and why?

Explicit replaces, narrows, confirms, and conflicts links must produce the right active set and an inspectable decision trail.

04 · RetrievalDid the right claims rank?

Relevance, diversity, multi-intent coverage, and evidence lineage are inspected only after factual authority is resolved.

05 · Context packingWhat did the model receive?

Mandatory facts and relationship decisions survive tight budgets; optional diagnostics are dropped first.

06 · GenerationDid the model obey?

This is scored separately. Correct context plus a bad answer is a model-adherence failure, not a hidden retrieval failure.

Synthetic relationship suite

Four meanings. Zero inference by vibes.

The deterministic suite uses harmless UI facts to test replaces, narrows, confirms, and conflicts. Replacement and conflict withhold the older statement while retaining its ID and history; narrowing and confirmation keep both claims active with explicit scoped instructions. Reverse-time links and missing targets are refused. Historical wording may retrieve its linked replacement, but the displaced statement is never injected as active truth. If a replacement later expires, its tombstone prevents the old claim from quietly resurrecting. Embeddings can rank surviving claims—they never decide what a relationship means.
